Terms & Conditions

Version 1.0 · effective 1 August 2026

1. Eligibility (21+)

You must be 21 years of age or older to open an account, deposit, or enter any pool. We collect your date of birth at registration and refuse accounts below this age.

2. Underage accounts may be banned

If we determine an account holder is under 21 — at registration or at any point afterwards — that account may be suspended or permanently banned without notice.

3. Withholding of funds

Where an account is found to be held by a person under 21, SportsPool reserves the right to withhold any funds in that account, including deposits, winnings and pending payouts, pending review.

4. Jurisdiction

Availability varies by jurisdiction. We verify eligibility using IP and billing address. Playing from a restricted jurisdiction, or misrepresenting your location, may result in closure and forfeiture.

5. Card privacy

Your parlay card and picks are visible only to you. No other player, and no pool owner, can view them. Discussing your specific picks in pool chat while a pool is open breaks these terms.
